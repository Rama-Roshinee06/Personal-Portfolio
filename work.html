About the Project

Bridging Tradition & Tech

While learning new technologies has always interested me, my true motivation lies in making them accessible to people who are unfamiliar with digital tools. This perspective led me to an internship with Selvalakshmi Travels, a pilgrimage-focused startup.I noticed that tour coordination relied heavily on manual calls, with the team often contacting many more people than required just to explain itineraries. To address this, I began by designing clear and engaging digital pamphlets, simplifying communication for pilgrims. I am now contributing to the development of an AI-powered website to help digitize operations, improve outreach, and support the organization’s growth—while keeping usability at the core.
